GrainForge

Digitalize and develop your film —
fast, affordable, and built for Mac.

GrainForge is a simple, streamlined, and affordable tool for digital film processing. Whether you're scanning old negatives from your parents or working with fresh film stock, GrainForge helps you digitalize and develop your images — tailored for speed and reproducibility with custom presets for every film type.

01
Negative Processing

From negative
to finished image

Transform your negative film scans with precision. Adjust the film base color to suit your specific film stock, fine-tune tone and tint to match your preferences, and save configurations as reusable presets — for both color and black-and-white film.

02
Photo Editing

Full control over
every scan

Adjust brightness, exposure, and contrast. Fine-tune color temperature and tint. Crop, mirror, or rotate to perfect your composition. Everything you need to refine your scans — nothing you don't.

03
Batch Processing

An entire roll
in seconds

Apply custom presets to entire galleries, transfer edits or crops from one image to all others in seconds, and export your finished photos in one go — perfectly processed and ready to share.
